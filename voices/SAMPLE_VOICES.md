# Sample Voice Sources

## Creating Your Own Voice Sample

### Option 1: Record Yourself
- Use QuickTime Player → File → New Audio Recording
- Speak naturally for 10-30 seconds
- Save as `my_voice.wav`

### Option 2: Voice Memos App
- Record on iPhone/Mac
- Share → Save to Files → `voices/my_voice.wav`

## Example Voice Prompts

Read one of these for consistent voice samples:

**Technology:**
"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Technology advances rapidly, and we must adapt. The future holds infinite possibilities for those who dare to dream."

**Storytelling:**
"In a world where voice becomes the bridge between human and machine, clarity is paramount. Every syllable matters, every pause breathes life into words."

**Mixed:**
"Hello, this is my voice sample. One, two, three, four. The weather today is pleasant. Artificial intelligence transforms how we communicate."

## Note on Copyright

Only use your own voice or voice samples you have permission to use.
Do not use voices from movies, games, or other copyrighted works.
